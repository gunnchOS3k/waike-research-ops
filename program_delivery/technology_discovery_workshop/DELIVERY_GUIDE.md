# Instructor delivery guide — Technology Discovery Workshop

Version 1.0.0 owner-review candidate. Two proposed 120-minute guided sessions. This is a bounded introduction, not all ten weeks of Digital Confidence. Its canonical instructional source is GENERAL_IT/delivery/digital_confidence.json at the curriculum revision in workshop.json; practical evaluation remains in the existing WAIKE evaluator. Do not author separate answers in this delivery guide.

## Before offering dates

A qualified lead instructor must rehearse every activity on the actual venue's devices and review the explanations, rubric and recovery steps. Confirm the buyer's objectives, adult audience, room/accessibility arrangements, allowed software, network access, support and reporting terms. A maximum of25 is a proposed service limit, not measured capacity. For25, plan a lead plus assistant and calculate available observation minutes: learners multiplied by observed minutes per learner must fit the assessment staffing window. Do not promise everyone an observed pass when the timetable cannot support it. Agree a smaller group, more staff or explicitly scheduled remediation/review before accepting a booking.

No production student account is required. Do not activate real school records or enroll minors to demonstrate the product. Use fictional notes and participant-selected non-identifying codes. Collect no passwords, serial numbers, screenshots with account names, recordings or personal files. Any retained attendance/evidence requires the buyer's approved notice, access rules and retention decision; this packet supplies none of those approvals.

## Technical preflight and materials

Supply the workbook and rubric, paper/pencils, one permitted device per learner or clearly assigned pair roles, a plain-text editor and an owned practice folder. Start from the workshop gateway, launch Digital Confidence and request a fresh unit1 case. Download, rename and reopen its fictional note; check selected file evidence. Confirm useful wrong-answer feedback, keyboard operation, text resizing, downloading evidence and returning to the gateway. Repeat unit4 restoration, unit6 arithmetic and the Week10 separate independent case. Cases expire after one hour, so request each when needed. A service restart or changed guide can invalidate a case: preserve files locally, request a new case and follow its new filenames/content. Never record a successful evaluation during an outage.

The local buyer demonstration may run on one controlled computer/projector. A25-device workshop needs an approved HTTPS host reachable by those devices; localhost on the instructor's machine is not their server. Verify allowed file downloads, available storage, blocked domains and low-bandwidth behavior. The current service is not accepted at25 concurrent learners. Rehearse the actual device/browser mix and agree a support fallback. Paper discussion and local file work can continue offline; server checks cannot. Do not substitute an invented score for an unavailable evaluator.

## Session1 — files, recovery and privacy (120minutes)

- 0–15: welcome, access needs and safety. Ask each learner to describe one everyday task. Explain files versus windows and a saved copy versus unsaved work. Demonstrate keyboard access and text zoom.
- 15–45: unit1. Model downloading the fictional original, finding the save location, creating an owned practice folder, renaming a working copy and reopening it. Learners repeat with their own issued cases. Swap pair roles so both operate the device. The checker verifies selected filename/text, not who performed the action.
- 45–55: introduce a controlled wrong copy and read the criterion feedback. Recover from the preserved original; do not delete the only copy. Ask learners to explain the difference between a shortcut and a separate editable copy.
- 55–65: break and individual access support.
- 65–95: unit4. Preserve an original in a backup folder, change a working copy, restore to a separate empty folder and verify the original text. Observe source/destination choices. A same-device practice copy is not protection against loss of that device.
- 95–110: unit5. Convert the issued minutes to seconds, distinguish observation from suspected cause and write a safe fictional ticket. Do not ask anyone to demonstrate a real password reset or expose an account.
- 110–120: learner explanation and recovery checkpoint. Save/download fictional practice evidence if desired. Record what still needs teaching; a practice pass is not mastery.

## Session2 — reasoning and independent demonstration (120minutes)

- 0–15: retrieve/reopen the prior fictional work. Revisit any filename, encoding or location difficulties without using a learner's personal files.
- 15–40: unit6. Work one capacity example from the canonical guide, explaining total, used, separate reserve and units. Learners solve new issued inputs; below-policy findings may be correct. Compare with the configured15% policy without deleting files to meet it.
- 40–55: unit10 handoff. Distinguish viewed, practiced, submitted, pending review and observed evidence. Show how a clear method, limitation and next action help a reviewer.
- 55–65: break, then request the separate fresh independent case in Week10. Explain permitted resources and rubric before work starts.
- 65–105: independent work and scheduled observations. Learners create renamed/restored case files, calculate the new capacity and explain a safe action and one failure/recovery. Use the rubric. Assistance for access is allowed and documented; supplying answers prevents an independence claim. Pending observations remain pending, even if bytes pass automatic checks.
- 105–120: formative debrief, evidence download, remediation instructions and next pathway. The instructor may need the agreed additional review window; report unresolved criteria explicitly. Do not force a pass to fit the session ending.

## Feedback, revision and evidence

Compare files and arithmetic through WAIKE's checks; observe file-manager/reopening behavior separately. Ask a learner to explain why their changed inputs differ from the example. Use “not observed”, “needs revision” and “observed” for each criterion in the companion rubric. Feedback names the mismatch and next safe action. Give a fresh case or changed inputs for revision; never paste the solution into a public bundle. An authorized institutional Hub can retain submissions/grades only after its independent deployment/privacy approvals. Guest downloads are unsubmitted evidence.

Return a basic aggregate report only from actual instructor observations. The offline assemble_report.py utility reads a local CSV and summarizes declared states; it neither authenticates the reviewer nor creates a school gradebook. It refuses duplicate participant codes and invalid states. No row or result is automatically invented. Report the counts, source revision, instructional scope, unresolved criteria, limitations and next steps. Do not infer learning gain without an appropriate genuine baseline/follow-up study.

## Instructor stop/recovery conditions

Stop collection if personal information appears. Help the learner remove it locally and follow the buyer's incident process for anything already shared. Pause a task if permission, physical access or accessibility support is missing. If a device's interface differs, find equivalent permitted file operations or provide an accessible teaching device; do not change administrator policies. When the evaluator fails, retain local fictional artifacts and mark checking unavailable. If the independent task exceeds the learner's prerequisites, teach the missing step and schedule a new demonstration. Keep the unsuccessful attempt and feedback as evidence of revision, not a hidden failure.

## Delivery boundary

This program supplies guided instruction, hands-on fictional practice and reviewed workshop-task evidence when an actual instructor completes review. It does not supply certification, accredited credit, employment guarantees, a full WAIKE course, a production institutional LMS, real6G measurements, book redistribution rights or a payment service. Related books may be shown only under their applicable clearance; unapproved editions remain in a separate owner-review environment. Proposed commercial pricing and staffing costs require validation and a scope agreement.

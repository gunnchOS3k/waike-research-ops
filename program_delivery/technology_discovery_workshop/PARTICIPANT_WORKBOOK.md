# Your Technology Discovery workbook

Use only fictional content. You need a browser, file manager and plain-text editor on a device you may use. A helper may assist with access; explain your own choices. Paper notes are welcome. Ask for a slower pace, larger text, keyboard support or an alternative permitted device when needed. Never share a password or personal file for this workshop.

## Session1

Open Digital Confidence unit1 from the workshop gateway. Request a case and download its fictional note. Find the file, put a working copy in your own practice folder, give it the required name, close the editor and reopen it. Choose that actual file for checking. Expected result: the filename and unchanged note match. If the check fails, compare the required name, text and extension; keep the original while repairing a copy.

Record privately: Where did you save the original? Which copy can you edit independently? What did reopening establish, and what could the browser not observe?

Open unit4 and request its new case. Preserve that original, change a working copy, then copy the preserved original into a separate restore folder. Use the required restored filename and check its text. Expected result: restored content matches the original, not the changed copy. A shortcut is not the restored bytes. Do not overwrite your only original. A copy on the same disk does not cover losing that disk.

In unit5, convert the issued minutes to seconds and distinguish a visible symptom from a suspected cause. Write a fictional ticket: task, observation, safe next test and uncertainty. Avoid credentials, names and private identifiers. A correct observation may still leave the cause unknown.

Produce the renamed and restored files, the calculation and your own explanation of method, one failure/recovery and a limitation. Use the lessons, a text editor, file manager and accessibility assistance. Do not copy a previous case's response. Ask the instructor to observe your actual file operations and explanation.

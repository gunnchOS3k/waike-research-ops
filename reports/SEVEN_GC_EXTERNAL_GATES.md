# SEVEN_GC_EXTERNAL_GATES

Digital scaffolding may include forms, rubrics, upload slots, safety checklists, and mentor-review placeholders.

## EXTERNAL_HUMAN_GATE
- Mentor approval / HUMAN_REVIEW completion
- Peer review worksheets (completion not auto-marked)
- Weeks 11–12 review/presentation sign-off

## EXTERNAL_PHYSICAL_GATE
- MEASURED_HARDWARE captures (SDR/device)
- Hardware EVT / pier measurement replacement of SIMULATED fixtures

## EXTERNAL_FIELD_GATE
- Campus/field pilots
- Partner participation evidence
- Cross-campus generalization claims

## Forbidden without evidence
- mentor_signed=true
- teacher approved / learner validated as completed
- hardware measured / field tested claims
- campus pilot complete

## Digital course may still complete when
- Labs use LOCAL_SOFTWARE / REPO_CONNECTED with SIMULATED or DIGITAL_REPRODUCTION evidence
- Capstone lists open EXTERNAL_* gates honestly

# Glossary — DATA VIZ BI

Track: `DATA_VIZ_BI`

## visual encoding
Mapping data fields to position, length, color, shape.

## chartjunk
Decorative ink that does not carry data.

## dashboard story
Ordered narrative of questions a viewer should answer.

## aggregation level
Grain at which numbers are summarized.

## annotation
Human-written context that prevents misreading a spike.

## export provenance
Source query/version behind a published chart.

## HITL narrative
Human reviews story text before release.

## contrast ratio
Accessibility measure for text/graphics against background.

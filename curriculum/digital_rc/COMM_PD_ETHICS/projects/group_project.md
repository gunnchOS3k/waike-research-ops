# Group project — COMM_PD_ETHICS (Harbor Desk Voice)

## Brief
Produce a **public-facing desk briefing** for a civic tech change (idle policy, printer outage, or research claim). One author drafts; one runs an ethics + accessibility review.

## Deliverables
1. 1-page briefing with audience, decision, claim boundary.
2. Accessibility notes (contrast, reading level, alt text plan).
3. AI disclosure log for drafting help.
4. HITL review checklist signed only if a real mentor reviewed (otherwise mark EXTERNAL_HUMAN_GATE).

## Rubric anchors
Clarity · Ethics honesty · Accessibility · No key leakage

## Explicit non-claims
Does not grant PMI/HR credentials; does not invent community endorsement.

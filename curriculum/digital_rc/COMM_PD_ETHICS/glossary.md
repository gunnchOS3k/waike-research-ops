# Glossary — COMM PD ETHICS

Track: `COMM_PD_ETHICS`

## claim boundary
Explicit statement of what evidence does and does not support.

## disclosure log
Record of AI or tool assistance used on graded work.

## stakeholder map
Who is affected by a decision and what they need to know.

## accessibility note
How a deliverable works for screen readers, keyboard, low bandwidth.

## conflict of interest
Personal or financial interest that must be disclosed before advising.

## informed consent
Permission after explaining purpose, risks, and data handling.

## HITL ethics gate
Human review before publishing claims that affect people.

## portfolio honesty
Artifacts show real work; no fabricated mentor signatures.

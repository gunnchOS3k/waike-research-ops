# Week 3: Professional tickets — no demeaning labels

Professional communication on Harbor Desk Voice is a ticket with a subject ≥8 characters and a body ≥80 characters that names observation and next action. Channel must be email, ticket, or slack_work — not personal SMS for desk work. demeaning_labels must be false. promises_outcome must be false: you do not guarantee the essay will return.

Ticket PD-2307 body that works: idle logout at 1200s discarded unsaved textarea on kiosk 3; check auto-save; coach Save As; no reboot-and-hope. Body that fails: 'you are stupid and lazy' — banned tokens include stupid, lazy, hopeless, idiot.

Consensus Ladder: observed = patron upset; inferred = session loss; still need = auto-save folder existence; action = restore path. Do not promise 'we will always recover every file.'

This is the week learners practice tone as a safety property: demeaning language escalates conflict and contaminates the evidence trail.

## Worked example

channel ticket; subject names kiosk; body ≥80 with restore path; demeaning_labels false; promises_outcome false.

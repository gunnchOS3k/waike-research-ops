# Week 6: Feedback rubric — evidence then score

Feedback on Harbor Desk Voice is criterion → evidence → score 0..4 → next_action, with identity_attack false. Ticket PD-2615 scores a peer journal on observation/inference separation. Evidence must quote the journal behavior, not the person's identity. Score 9 fails. next_action must tell the learner what to try next.

Demeaning feedback is an ethics failure even when the score is numerically correct. 'You always mess up because of who you are' is identity_attack and fails.

Consensus Ladder: observed = journal text; inferred = separation quality; still need = one improvement; action = next_action line. Do not publish grades without human-in-the-loop when acting as educator copilot. Harbor Desk Voice insists on numbered ticket IDs in journals so a Saturday volunteer can continue without a hallway interrogation. Write the next action as a verb phrase a stranger can execute. Keep patron faces, passwords, and invented harm counts out of the packet. If two tools disagree, write what you saw, then what you guessed, then the missing fact before changing shared systems. Professional development here is measured by reproducible desk artifacts, not by slogans about culture.

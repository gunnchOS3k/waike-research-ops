# Week 2: Conflict of interest — disclose and recuse

Harbor Desk Voice treats conflict as a scheduling problem, not a moral panic. Ticket PD-2204: a Saturday volunteer mentors classmate Jordan on the portfolio while also sitting on the scoring panel for that same portfolio. Mentoring is fine. Scoring after private coaching is not. The fix is disclose_to=course lead and recuse=true when conflict_present=true.

Write the scenario in ≥40 characters with concrete roles. Rationale must explain the unfair advantage without attacking Jordan's identity. 'It's fine' is not a rationale. Recusal without disclosure still fails the desk culture: the lead needs to reassign the scorer.

Consensus Ladder: observed = same person coaches and scores; inferred = grading advantage; still need = alternate scorer name; action = recuse and hand off. Do not invent injury statistics or claim accreditation from a recusal drill.

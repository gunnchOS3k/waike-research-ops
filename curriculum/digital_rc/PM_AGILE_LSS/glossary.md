# Glossary — PM AGILE LSS

Track: `PM_AGILE_LSS`

## WIP limit
Maximum work-in-progress allowed on a board column.

## definition of done
Checklist that must be true before an item is complete.

## SIPOC
Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers map.

## control chart
Plot of a process metric with control limits.

## risk register
List of risks with owners and mitigations.

## stakeholder RACI
Responsible/Accountable/Consulted/Informed matrix.

## HITL gate
Human decision point in a workflow (e.g., go/no-go).

## retrospective
Team meeting to improve process based on evidence.

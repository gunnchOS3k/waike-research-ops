# Glossary — Cybersecurity / CYBER_SOC

- **CIA**: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.
- **IAM / RBAC**: Identity and access; roles bind actions.
- **SIEM**: Log aggregation and alerting (Harbor uses fixture JSON).
- **Containment**: Stop spread before eradicate/wipe.
- **Least privilege**: Bots read; humans close.
- **Chain of custody**: Who touched evidence JSON, when, hash.
- **Phishing defense**: Report and quarantine — never harvest credentials as 'practice.'
- **Sandbox**: Course CTF fixture only; no live targets you do not own.

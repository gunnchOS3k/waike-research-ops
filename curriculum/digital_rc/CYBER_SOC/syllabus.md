# Cybersecurity Foundations and SOC Readiness — Night Shift Desk

## Who this is for
CYBER_SOC COURSE_DIGITAL_RC: analyst-desk numeracy for a junior SOC seat — log parsing, windowed detection, precision and recall, indicator hygiene, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, email authentication, CVSS v3.1, triage queues, and shift metrics. Defensive only, synthetic logs, documentation IP ranges. Distinct from the CYBERSECURITY Harbor SOC governance course. Not student/teacher E6.

## Tracks and academy
- Tracks: CYBER_SOC
- Academy: ACADEMY_CYBER

## Duration
Ten desk weeks at 6–8 hours each, one simulated overnight queue per week. Weeks 3 (precision and recall) and 7 (CVSS by hand) are NO_AI.

## Weekly map
- Week 01: Auth log anatomy — failures, sources, and the success that follows
- Week 02: Windowed detection — a threshold means nothing without a clock
- Week 03: Detection quality — precision, recall, and the analyst's hour
- Week 04: Indicator hygiene — hash it, defang it, then compare
- Week 05: ATT&CK mapping — describe behaviour, not villains
- Week 06: Email authentication — passing is not the same as aligned
- Week 07: CVSS v3.1 by hand — from vector string to severity band
- Week 08: Triage queue — severity meets asset criticality
- Week 09: Shift metrics — MTTD, MTTR, and the median that resists one bad night
- Week 10: Shift handoff — open incidents, NIST phases, and clean boundaries

## Assessments
Night Shift Desk grading: weekly SOC quizzes on log reading, windowed rules, precision and recall, indicator hygiene, ATT&CK mapping, DMARC alignment, CVSS v3.1, queue order, and shift metrics; a 20-item midterm on detection arithmetic; a 24-item final on triage judgement and handoff; ten recomputing desk labs; a written shift handoff reviewed by the incoming analyst.

## Claim boundary
MITRE ATT&CK, FIRST CVSS v3.1, NIST SP 800-61r2, and RFC 7489 are PUBLIC_REFERENCE_ONLY. Synthetic logs and documentation IP ranges only; defensive practice, no live targets. Not a SOC certification. COURSE_DIGITAL_RC only, not REAL_STUDENT_E6 or REAL_TEACHER_E6.

## Kinesthetic hook
Ten Night Shift Desk tickets: read the auth log → tune a window rule → measure precision → hash and defang indicators → map to ATT&CK → check DMARC alignment → score CVSS by hand → order the queue → report MTTD/MTTR → hand off the shift.

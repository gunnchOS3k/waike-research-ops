# Glossary — ROBOTICS CONTROL

Track: `ROBOTICS_CONTROL`

## closed-loop control
Using feedback to correct toward a setpoint.

## PID
Proportional-integral-derivative controller structure.

## workspace
Reachable set of poses for a manipulator.

## estop
Emergency stop that removes motive power.

## odometry
Pose estimate from motion sensors; drifts without correction.

## sim-to-real gap
Difference between simulation behavior and physical robots.

## HITL enable
Human enables motors after safety checklist.

## safety enclosure
Physical barrier / light curtain requirements for motion.

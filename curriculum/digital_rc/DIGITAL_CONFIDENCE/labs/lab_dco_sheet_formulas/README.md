# lab_dco_sheet_formulas — Spreadsheet formulas — SUM, AVERAGE, MAX, share of total

Enter a budget of at least four rows, then report total, average, the largest item, and one item's share as a percentage.

Try it, then change one number in your file and check that the answer moves:

python3 scripts/run_course_labs.py --lab lab_dco_sheet_formulas --submission path/to/student.json

Common miss: Typing a total by hand that disagrees with the rows fails.

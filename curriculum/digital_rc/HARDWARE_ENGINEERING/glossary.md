# Glossary — HARDWARE ENGINEERING

Track: `HARDWARE_ENGINEERING`

## schematic
Symbolic representation of electrical connections.

## BOM
Bill of materials listing parts, quantities, and sources.

## ESD
Electrostatic discharge; handled with grounded practices.

## tolerance
Allowed variation in a component value or dimension.

## DFx
Design for manufacturability/test/assembly considerations.

## bring-up log
Power sequence and measurements during first board test.

## HITL power-on
Human confirms polarity/voltage before applying power.

## lab vs field
Bench measurements are not automatic field qualification.

# Week 10: Research capstone — traced figures, no submission claim

SGA-1A10 assembles the packet that cohorts on different campuses exchange. It contains the week-one claim ledger, updated with what the term actually showed, and at least one figure. Every figure carries the config digest and seed from its manifest, so a reader in Nubuke can regenerate the Gary figure without emailing anyone. The packet names its evidence class, which for this course is SYNTHETIC_SIM end to end, and repeats the week-eight gap in plain words. It also states what it is not: submitted_claim stays false, because drafting a figure for the research spine is not the same as submitting a paper, and only the program owner decides when a manuscript goes out. Mentors review the packet against six or more passed labs, check that no instructor answer key leaked into the learner folder, and rerun one figure from its manifest in front of the apprentice. A packet that survives that rerun is a real research artifact at apprentice scale. It is not a publication, a credential, or evidence of deployment, and the closing slide says so.

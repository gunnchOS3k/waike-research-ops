# Networking and Internet Infrastructure — Backbone Room

## Who this is for
NETWORKING_INFRA COURSE_DIGITAL_RC: internet-scale infrastructure arithmetic — fiber propagation, IPv6 text and prefix math, bandwidth-delay product, BGP path selection, DNS caching, CDN offload, optical link budgets, availability in series and parallel, and 95th-percentile transit billing. Documentation ASNs and prefixes only. Distinct from COMPUTER_NETWORKING (campus LAN, subnetting, switching). Not student/teacher E6.

## Tracks and academy
- Tracks: NETWORKING_INFRA
- Academy: ACADEMY_NETWORKING

## Duration
Ten change-request weeks at 6–8 hours each, paper first then spreadsheet. Weeks 7 (optical budget) and 9 (transit bill) are NO_AI.

## Weekly map
- Week 01: Fiber latency budget — light is slower in glass
- Week 02: IPv6 text and prefixes — one canonical way to write an address
- Week 03: Bandwidth-delay product — why one connection cannot fill the pipe
- Week 04: BGP best path — policy first, then the shorter story
- Week 05: DNS TTL — planning a change the world sees late
- Week 06: CDN offload — the miss ratio is what the origin pays for
- Week 07: Optical link budget — loss, margin, and the 3 dB rule
- Week 08: Availability — redundancy in parallel, single points in series
- Week 09: 95th-percentile billing — the bursts you do not pay for
- Week 10: Backbone design packet — documentation numbers, recomputed

## Assessments
Backbone Room grading: weekly checks on fiber latency, IPv6 text, bandwidth-delay product, BGP path choice, DNS TTL planning, CDN offload, optical budgets, availability, and 95th-percentile billing; a 20-item midterm on infrastructure arithmetic; a 24-item final on design trade-offs; ten recomputing change-request labs; a design packet built only from documentation numbers.

## Claim boundary
IETF RFCs 4271, 5952, 7323, and 1034 and Cisco CCNA labels are PUBLIC_REFERENCE_ONLY. Documentation ASNs and the 2001:db8::/32 prefix only; no production peering claim. COURSE_DIGITAL_RC only, not REAL_STUDENT_E6 or REAL_TEACHER_E6.

## Kinesthetic hook
Ten Backbone Room change requests: budget fiber latency → write IPv6 canonically → size TCP windows → pick a BGP path → plan a DNS cutover → size CDN offload → budget an optical span → compute availability → read a 95th-percentile bill → submit a design packet.

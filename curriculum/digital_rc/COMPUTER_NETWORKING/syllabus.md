# Computer Networking — Packets to Campus Edge

## Who this is for
Ten weeks on the WAIKE Packet Range: chop packets, survey CIDR, learn MACs with VLANs, forward with TTL and longest prefix, number TCP bytes, break loops, run SPF, interrogate DHCP/DNS/NAT liars, write an ordered ACL, then ship an intent file. Cisco CCNA 200-301 v1.1 domain weights are labels. Stanford CS144 is a structure citation. No Cisco labs, no Stanford code, no exam PDFs.

## Tracks and academy
- Tracks: NETWORKING_INFRA
- Academy: ACADEMY_NETWORKING

## Duration
Ten Packet Range weeks with one deep data-path practical in week 4 that returns in the capstone. Budget a quiet table for hex slicing; this is not a GUI click-through.

## Weekly map
- Week 01: Packets are chopped on purpose
- Week 02: CIDR as a land survey, not a guessing game
- Week 03: The MAC closet — learning, flooding, and VLAN 20
- Week 04: Forwarding plane — TTL, LPM, and a crafted IPv4 frame
- Week 05: Reliability on an unreliable wire — sequences, ACKs, AIMD on paper
- Week 06: When VLANs meet a loop — STP as a circuit breaker
- Week 07: Four-router town — SPF beats the scenic route
- Week 08: DHCP, DNS, NAT — services that lie for us on purpose
- Week 09: ACLs that actually order, and the telnet we refuse
- Week 10: Campus edge capstone — intent files and a datapath proof

## Assessments
Packet Range assessment mix: weekly protocol quizzes, a mid-course written exam on encapsulation/CIDR/LPM/TCP numbering (20 original items), a final on STP/SPF/NAT/ACL/intent files (24 original items), a practical that parses a crafted Ethernet+IPv4 frame and must drop a real TTL=1 header, and a guest-VLAN redesign presentation. A trace that omits VLAN, LPM, TTL, or ACL is incomplete.

## Claim boundary
Aligns to Cisco CCNA 200-301 v1.1 domain weights and cites CS144's weekly shape. Does not grant CCNA and does not ship Stanford code or solutions. Instructor keys stay out of the learner packet.

## Kinesthetic hook
Parse a crafted frame, run LPM, and prove a four-router town on paper then in code.

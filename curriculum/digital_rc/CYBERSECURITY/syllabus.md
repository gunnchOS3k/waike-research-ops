# Cybersecurity — Harbor SOC Foundations

## Who this is for
Harbor SOC is a classroom SOC. Prefer ISC2 CC outline effective 2026-09-01 (principles, governance, IAM, network/cloud, ops+IR) with AI woven through. Dual-map current CC and Security+ SY0-701. Berkeley CS161 donates a depth pattern only. All security labs are authorized fixtures. No dumps, no unauthorized scanning.

## Tracks and academy
- Tracks: CYBER_SOC
- Academy: ACADEMY_CYBER

## Duration
Ten Harbor SOC weeks. Week 8 is the only vulnerability lab and it stays on the length-prefixed toy parser. Do not schedule 'scan the campus' as homework.

## Weekly map
- Week 01: Harbor SOC — principles, governance, and the model that is also an asset
- Week 02: Identity lifecycle — Naiya, Omar, and harbor-bot
- Week 03: Cloud-shaped edges and the kiosk that must not walk into the SOC
- Week 04: SIEM triage — bursts are a look, not a conviction
- Week 05: Hardening the image — guest off, keys on, bot sudo off
- Week 06: Segmentation that survives a stolen laptop
- Week 07: Incident clock — detect, contain, eradicate, recover, lessons
- Week 08: Authorized toy parser — detect the length lie, do not grow an exploit kit
- Week 09: USB story — a timeline is an argument
- Week 10: Harbor capstone — evidence locker design then operate

## Assessments
Harbor SOC assessment mix: weekly ops quizzes, a mid-course on upcoming ISC2 CC 2026-09-01 domains in WAIKE words (20 original items), a final on hardening/IR/toy-parser/forensics (24 original items), a practical over seven fixture labs that reject empty submissions, and an evidence-locker design checkpoint before the policy checker. Burst notes that say 'attacker' fail. Scanning hosts you do not own fails the course, not just the lab.

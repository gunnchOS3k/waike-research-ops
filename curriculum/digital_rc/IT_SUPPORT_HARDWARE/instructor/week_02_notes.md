# IT_SUPPORT_HARDWARE — Bench-lead notes, week 2

**Board work:** DDR4-3200 in A2 + B2 → 2 channels → 3200 × 8 × 2 / 1000 = 51.2 GB/s; in A1 + A2 → 1 channel → 25.6 GB/s

**Hands on the case:** have each technician run lab_ish_memory_bandwidth against the machine on their bench.

**Watch for:** Two sticks on the same channel letter; firmware shows single-channel.

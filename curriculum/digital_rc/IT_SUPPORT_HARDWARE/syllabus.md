# IT Support and Hardware Foundations — Refurb Bench

## Who this is for
IT_SUPPORT_HARDWARE COURSE_DIGITAL_RC: bench-technician arithmetic and decision tables for refurbishing donated computers — power budgets, memory channels, RAID, partition schemes, fault isolation, display bandwidth, UPS runtime, imaging throughput, and media sanitization. Distinct from GENERAL_IT (tickets and operating systems) and HARDWARE_ENGINEERING (circuits and embedded design). Not student/teacher E6.

## Tracks and academy
- Tracks: IT_SUPPORT_HARDWARE
- Academy: ACADEMY_IT

## Duration
Ten bench weeks at 6–8 hours each with a donated machine on the bench. Weeks 3 (RAID table) and 9 (sanitization) are NO_AI.

## Weekly map
- Week 01: Power supply budget — parts, headroom, and the smallest adequate unit
- Week 02: Memory channels — the slots you choose set the bandwidth
- Week 03: RAID — usable space, tolerated failures, and why it is not a backup
- Week 04: MBR or GPT — the two-terabyte ceiling and UEFI boot
- Week 05: Fault isolation — symptom first, subsystem second, parts last
- Week 06: Display bandwidth — will the cable carry the mode?
- Week 07: UPS runtime — watt-hours, inverter loss, and a graceful shutdown
- Week 08: Imaging a lab — unicast one by one versus one multicast stream
- Week 09: Media sanitization — Clear, Purge, or Destroy before a device leaves
- Week 10: Refurb service report — fault, fix, sanitization, proof

## Assessments
Refurb Bench grading: weekly checks on power budgets, memory channels, RAID, partition schemes, fault isolation, display bandwidth, UPS runtime, imaging time, and sanitization; a 20-item midterm on bench arithmetic; a 24-item final on diagnosis and data-handling judgement; ten recomputing bench labs; a per-device service report with sanitization proof.

## Claim boundary
CompTIA A+ objective labels, NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, and the UEFI specification are PUBLIC_REFERENCE_ONLY; no exam items. Donor data is never opened. Not A+ certification or a manufacturer warranty authority. COURSE_DIGITAL_RC only, not REAL_STUDENT_E6 or REAL_TEACHER_E6.

## Kinesthetic hook
Ten Refurb Bench work orders on donated PCs: size the PSU → populate memory channels → plan RAID → choose MBR or GPT → isolate a fault → match display and cable → size a UPS → image a lab → sanitize drives → file the service report.

# Week 10: Refurb service report — fault, fix, sanitization, proof

ISH-4A10 closes the term with the document that travels with each refurbished machine. For every device the report gives the serial number, the fault class from the bench's isolation table, and the fix in plain words, such as reseated the memory stick in A2. It records the sanitization method chosen for the drive and that verification passed, pointing at last week's certificate. It notes anything a new owner should know, like a replacement power supply, the memory mode confirmed in firmware, or a monitor that needs the DisplayPort cable for full refresh. The bench also signs one sentence for the whole batch: no customer data was retained, copied, or looked through beyond what the repair required. Technicians who browse a donor's photos to 'test the drive' end their volunteering that day. Mentors review the report against six or more passed labs, pick one machine at random, and ask the technician to explain the fault and the fix while the case is open. A clean report is what lets a school trust a donated computer.

## Worked example

RB-1002: fault memory_or_cpu_seating, fix reseated DIMM in A2, sanitization Purge, verified; customer_data_retained false

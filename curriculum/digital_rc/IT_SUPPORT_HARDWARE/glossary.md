# Glossary — IT Support and Hardware Foundations

Track: `IT_SUPPORT_HARDWARE` (shares GENERAL_IT modules)

## hardware triage
Ordered diagnosis: power, storage, memory, then OS.

## ESD
Electrostatic discharge controls when handling components.

## bring-up
First validation after power or parts change.

## content_ref
Pointer into GENERAL_IT modules without duplication.

## HITL
Human confirmation before privileged apply/flash/image write.

## spare parts discipline
Do not consume the only DIMM before naming the fault.

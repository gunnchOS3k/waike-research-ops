# Glossary — GENERAL IT

Track: `GENERAL_IT`

## idle logout
Automatic session end after inactivity to protect shared kiosks.

## least privilege
Accounts hold only the groups needed for the ticket type.

## 3-2-1 backup
Three copies, two media types, one off-box copy.

## change window
Approved time range for applying operational changes.

## ticket severity
Impact-based priority, not volume of adjectives.

## link-local
169.254/16 address indicating DHCP failure on IPv4.

## HITL sudo
Human judgment before elevating privileges on shared machines.

## Consensus Ladder
Observed / inferred / still-need discipline for desk notes.

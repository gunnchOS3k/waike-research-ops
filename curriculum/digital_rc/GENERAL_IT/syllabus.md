# General IT — Operator to Support Specialist

## Who this is for
This is not a survey of 'computers.' It is the job of keeping a public desk alive: a library kiosk that logs people out at 20:00 idle, a printer that only answers to a local name, and a Friday change that must snapshot before it mutates. Weeks 1–4 build operator muscle (Digital Confidence). Weeks 5–10 add hardware triage, services, DNS, backup, and automation (IT Support and Hardware). Google IT Support module titles and CompTIA A+ V15 220-1201/1202 domain names are alignment labels only.

## Tracks and academy
- Tracks: DIGITAL_CONFIDENCE, IT_SUPPORT_HARDWARE
- Academy: ACADEMY_IT

## Duration
10 weeks. Operator/support, packet-range, or Harbor SOC hours — not a 2-hour workshop pretending to be a course.

## Weekly map
- Week 01: The Civic Tech Desk — three jobs a computer actually has
- Week 02: Two operating systems, one pair of hands
- Week 03: Users, groups, and the sudo you can actually type
- Week 04: Storage, snapshots, and the Friday 16:00 panic
- Week 05: Tickets are promises, not chat threads
- Week 06: Hardware triage — power, then storage, then memory, then OS
- Week 07: Names on the LAN — hosts, a tiny zone, and the printer that only exists locally
- Week 08: Services, printers, and the tracker that must stay dead
- Week 09: Automation without heroics — snapshot, change window, rollback
- Week 10: After-hours capstone — keep the desk alive without becoming a hero

## Assessments
- 10 weekly quizzes (6 original items each)
- Mid-course knowledge (20 items, weeks 1–5)
- Final knowledge (24 items)
- Final practical (runnable labs)
- Group project / capstone

## Kinesthetic hook
Run the Gary Civic Tech Desk for ten weeks: accounts, disks, tickets, then an after-hours change window.

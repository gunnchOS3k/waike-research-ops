# Glossary — CLOUD DEVOPS

Track: `CLOUD_DEVOPS`

## immutable artifact
Build output identified by digest; redeployed rather than patched in place.

## rollback window
Time-bounded plan to restore last-known-good after a failed change.

## SLO
Service level objective — numeric reliability/latency target with error budget.

## CI gate
Automated check that must pass before merge or promote.

## secret hygiene
Keeping credentials out of repos, logs, and learner packets.

## blue/green
Two environments with traffic cutover for safer releases.

## observability
Logs, metrics, and traces sufficient to answer 'what broke?' without guessing.

## HITL promote
Human approval before production promotion even when CI is green.

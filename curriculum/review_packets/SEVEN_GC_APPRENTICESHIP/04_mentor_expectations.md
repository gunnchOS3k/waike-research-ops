# Mentor expectations

## Digital course (weeks 1–10)
Mentors are **optional**. Digital completion does **not** require mentor_signed=true.

## Apprenticeship extension (weeks 11–12 scaffolds)
When a live mentor is assigned under EXTERNAL_HUMAN_GATE:
- Hold scheduled check-ins per `apprenticeships/mentor_check_in_model.md`.
- Review claim audits and refuse field-upgrade fiction.
- Sign only after reviewing digests + limitations; never sign blank templates.
- Document still-need items (hardware, spectrum, IRB if human subjects appear).

## What mentors must not do
- Rubber-stamp portfolios without artifacts.
- Ask learners to invent MEASURED_FIELD results for deadlines.
- Share instructor answer keys with learners.

## Gate status
`SEVEN_GC_APPRENTICESHIP_HUMAN_ACADEMIC_REVIEW_PASS=false` until academic reviewer completes checklist.
Mentor field completion remains separately gated.

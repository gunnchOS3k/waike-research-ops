# Objectives — 7GC AI-RAN Research Apprenticeship

## Program intent
Prepare learners to practice **reproducible wireless/systems research methods** on labeled digital fixtures, with honest evidence classes and open EXTERNAL gates for mentor, hardware, and field work.

## Learning objectives (digital course)
1. Name and apply evidence classes: SIMULATED, DIGITAL_REPRODUCTION, MEASURED_HARDWARE, MEASURED_FIELD, HUMAN_REVIEW.
2. Perform repository reconnaissance without inventing sibling-repo SHAs.
3. Reproduce a SIMULATED KPI baseline with command, environment, and digest.
4. Author an experiment design (question, IV/DV, controls, confounds) before running trials.
5. Execute a one-IV controlled simulation and preserve raw artifacts.
6. Separate vendor/marketing claims from fixture-supported results; keep `commercial_6g_exists=false`.
7. Complete a claim audit listing supports vs does-not-support.
8. Assemble a digital capstone package with `mentor_signed=false` unless EXTERNAL_HUMAN_GATE completes.
9. Map ≥3 research-repo roles from `knowledge_maps/course_repo_map.yaml` with UNAVAILABLE allowed.
10. Run a sensitivity/robustness sweep without inventing field gains.

## Non-objectives (explicit)
- Not a 3GPP/O-RAN membership path.
- Not a guarantee of employment, publication acceptance, or campus pilot authority.
- Not authorization to transmit RF or access production RAN.

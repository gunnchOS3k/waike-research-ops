# Research methods

## Method stance
Learners practice **experimental research literacy** on deterministic fixtures before any hardware.

## Required practices
- Pre-register design fields (question, hypothesis, IV, DV, controls, confounds) before week-5 run.
- Record provenance: command, cwd, python/runtime version, seed, output SHA-256.
- Separate observation (fixture values) from inference (causal stories).
- Prefer one independent variable per controlled run.
- Sensitivity analysis varies named controls (e.g., `snr_db`) and reports stability honestly.
- Negative results and UNAVAILABLE repos are valid outcomes.

## Consensus Ladder (required language)
- **Observed** — labeled fixture row, digest, or log line.
- **Inferred** — interpretation that goes beyond the bytes.
- **Still need** — named EXTERNAL gate or additional measurement.

## Forbidden method fiction
- Relabeling SIMULATED as MEASURED_FIELD.
- Inventing mentor signatures or pier BLER curves.
- Mutating production infrastructure for coursework.

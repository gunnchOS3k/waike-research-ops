# Assessment plan

## Components
- **Weekly quizzes** q01–q10 (6 items each; 60 total) — evidence literacy.
- **Mid-course** — 20 original items on digital honesty / gates.
- **Final knowledge** — 24 original items on EXTERNAL gates + claim discipline.
- **Practical** — runnable labs (10); empty/wrong fail.
- **Project / portfolio** — digital capstone package + claim audit.

## AI modes
- Default weekly: AI_DISCLOSED.
- NO_AI: weeks 4 (design) and 7 (claim audit quiz).
- AI never silently grades; keys in `instructor/answer_keys.json` only.

## Pass philosophy
Digital course can pass on strong SIMULATED packages that list physical follow-ons.
Invented mentor/field completion fails regardless of quiz scores.

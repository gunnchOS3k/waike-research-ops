# Publication / portfolio expectations

## Portfolio (required for digital completion)
- Capstone package + claim audit
- Lab digests and AI disclosure log
- Career map excerpt (roles aligned, not granted)
- Accessibility notes excerpt

## Publication (aspirational, not required)
- Workshop abstract or campus poster using **only** supported claims
- Open artifact badge aspiration (ACM) — not auto-awarded
- No submission of fabricated field results

## Honest CV language examples
- Good: "Reproduced SIMULATED AI-RAN KPI baseline and documented claim limits."
- Bad: "Deployed 6G AI-RAN on Gary pier" (unsupported).

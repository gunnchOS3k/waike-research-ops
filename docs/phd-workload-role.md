# WAIKE PhD Workload Role

## Framing

WAIKE is the human learning, workforce, and community operating model that generates realistic service requirements for the 6G device ecosystem.

**WAIKE is not the dissertation by itself.**
**WAIKE does not replace the wireless/network research problem.**
**The full curriculum is not required before application submission.**

WAIKE is the use-case generator that makes the research workloads meaningful and grounded in genuine educational and community contexts.

## What WAIKE provides to the PhD

1. **Realistic workload profiles** — activity patterns grounded in human learning, creation, and community contexts
2. **Device diversity justification** — different activities naturally map to different device form factors
3. **Connectivity stress diversity** — learning activities span sustained sessions, mobile interactions, local creation, and embodied sensing
4. **Quality-of-experience grounding** — service continuity metrics are meaningful because they affect real educational outcomes
5. **Digital-equity framing** — the research addresses connectivity for underserved communities

## What WAIKE does NOT provide

- The scientific research contribution (that is the 6G service-continuity method)
- The evaluation methodology (that comes from simulation and measurement)
- The technical novelty (that comes from the connectivity architecture)
- Validated educational outcomes (no pilot data exists)
- Proof of curriculum effectiveness (not claimed)

## Relationship to non-negotiables

- WAIKE must not overtake the Communications Engineering focus
- WAIKE must not turn the application into an education PhD
- WAIKE must justify workloads, service requirements, and impact context
- No learner data may be collected without ethics review
- No minors or school pilots may be implied as guaranteed
- No claims of impact without evidence
- Curriculum incompleteness disclosed as draft/under development
